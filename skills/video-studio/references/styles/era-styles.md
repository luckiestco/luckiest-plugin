# Era Styles (history speedruns, time travel, tributes)

- id: `era-styles`
- Source: huashu-art-motion `INDEX.md` era table and recipes (MIT)

These 18 looks are tied to one period or one artist and read as a costume on other topics. Use them for art-history speedruns (the style changes, the scene stays), time-travel montages, "a brief history of X", and tributes. For any other subject use a material-layer card instead (impressionist, cut-paper, ink-wash, comic-pop and the rest). Each era runs 0.25-1.4 s inside a speedrun, so the motif motion must read inside that window. Aging marks (peeling, stains, scratches) go on the background only, never on the subject. Compose each film for its own subject; the source samples all share one fixed room layout, which is a property of that film and not of the styles.

Quality ratings come from the source (3 stars = recognizable on sight, 2 = recognizable with a visible gap, 1 = redo).
