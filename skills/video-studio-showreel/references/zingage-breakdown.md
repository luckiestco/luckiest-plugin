# Zingage showreel 1.0: breakdown

Source: whatships.com/videos/zingage (Blissful 2026 showreel series), 84.4 s,
1920x1080, 30 fps. Studied from 2 fps contact sheets on 2026-09-30. Structure
and motion only; none of its footage, logo, or copy is reused.

**Structure:** mark born, wordmark, dive into the site, scroll tour, orange field
with exploded UI cards, devices and cursor, behind-the-scenes, headline,
culture board, people masked into the mark, kinetic line, bookend. 2 hard cuts
(14.7 s, 50.0 s); everything else is a continuous move.

**Pacing:** dense at both ends (a transform every 0.5-1 s), slow in the middle
(UI tour holds 10 s), second burst in the orange chapter.

**Signature moves to keep:** orb to mosaic burst; blueprint dashed draw-on;
3D-extruded UI with depth of field; close to wide reveal; slabs lifting off a
page; photos masked inside the mark; italic accent word in brand color; loop
bookend.

**Do not copy:** the footage, the Zingage mark and wordmark, its copy, its
photos, its music.
