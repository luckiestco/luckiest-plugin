# Changelog — luckiest-video-studio-recreate

## 1.3.0 — 2026-10-08
- Step 1 runs `luckiest-video-watcher` in study mode for the transcript and sound
  notes that feed each shot's audio and on-screen text.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-08
- Step 4a locks repeated people and sets before any video: a character sheet, an
  empty set plate, one approved first look, then start frames per shot on a
  contact sheet. Shots animate from their start frames with the sheet as reference.
- Step 4c drafts every clip cheaply and finalizes only approved, on-screen takes.
- Practice studied from the PD House motion guide (pdhouse.notion.site/motion-guide). Ideas only, no prompts copied.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-05
- Carries the art director's look into every shot prompt when installed.

## 1.0.1 — 2026-10-03
- Points at `luckiest-video-studio-composition` (when installed) for focal point, grid, white space, and safe areas.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-01
- New sub-skill: URL or mp4 to a per-shot prompt list (`prompts.md`,
  `prompts.json`), AI generation through the creative MCP with a cost check
  first, concatenation, and a shot-by-shot comparison against the source.
- `--prompts-only` stops after the prompts for use in any video tool.
