# Changelog — luckiest-brandkit-sizing

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-07
- New sub-skill of luckiest-brandkit for real-world logo size and placement.
- Apparel table (left chest, center chest, full front, upper back, full back,
  back neck, sleeve, hoodie, youth) in inches, cm, and share of chest width,
  plus hats, bags, and embroidery limits.
- How to state the size in a shot's COMPOSITION line and how to check it in the
  image (share of chest width, plus or minus 5 points).
- `references/signage-and-print.md`: minimum size, clear space, business card
  and letterhead, mockup scale, and signage letter height by viewing distance.
- Built from Wooter, Rush Order Tees, ThreadLogic, Printful, Signs.com, and
  brand guide research, read 2026-10-07.
