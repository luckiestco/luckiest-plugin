# Attribution

luckiest-usertest adapts the method from Angus Sewell ("Angus the
Nontechnical"):

- the short video "Vibe code a fake customer army"
  (https://www.facebook.com/reel/1158451790077266), watched 2026-11-04
- the free guide "IShowSpeed's Funnel Testing Playbook"
  (vault.angussewell.com/resources/106), read 2026-11-05

Taken from the guide: the six-stage loop (check access, find buyer types, walk
the route, fix on a preview, repeat until the route holds, measure real
behavior), the clear / friction / blocked labels, the one-row-per-reproducible-
problem rule and the found > fixing > retest > closed status, the two-clean-rounds
or five-rounds stop rule, and the counting rules for real funnel data (browser
journeys, largest count loss vs largest percent loss).

The guide's prompts are not reproduced. The wording, safety rules, file layout,
card format, wrong-fit extra tester, and the text-only walk caveat in this skill
are (c) luckiest. The guide is inspired by an IShowSpeed video and is not
affiliated with IShowSpeed. Neither is this skill.
