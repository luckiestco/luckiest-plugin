# Attribution

`luckiest-video-studio-script` is a Luckiest sub-skill of `luckiest-video-studio`.

The five-part, three-act structure, the 90-second and word limits, the SUCCESS
checks, and the one-smile rule follow the public teaching of Miguel Hernandez of
Grumo Media: the post "5 Parts Every Explainer Video Script Needs"
(grumomedia.com), his Entrepreneurs HQ, Mixergy, and Business Growth interviews,
and the GRUMO YouTube video "Script Writing: Boring vs. Cute". SUCCESS adapts the
"Made to Stick" framework by Chip and Dan Heath. Nothing was copied from his paid
course, and all wording here is original.

The per-act timing and the words-per-second figures were measured by Luckiest on
2026-10-02 from the timestamped captions of 11 Grumo explainers on the GRUMO
YouTube channel. Part boundaries were marked by hand to about ±2 seconds. They
describe those videos, not a rule Grumo has published.
