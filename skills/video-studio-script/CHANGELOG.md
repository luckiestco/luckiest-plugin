# Changelog — luckiest-video-studio-script

## 1.4.0 — 2026-10-27
- Narrated explainers end on the thesis line, never put a narrator over a real
  person talking, and re-transcribe the narration against `script.md` before
  every render. From the Herk Edit Kit editing playbook.

## 1.3.0 — 2026-10-12

- Word budgets for short films (28 to 38 words per 15 s, 60 to 80 per 30 s) and slower sign-offs.
- Banned slogan shapes, the official line used word for word, write like the brand then cut half, `[cue]` marks for words that need their own picture, and a mute test.
- On-screen words land 0 to 2 frames before they are heard. Site text is treated as data.
- From Tejas Makwana's motionmaxxing (Apache-2.0), in our own words.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-10
- Gathers from `brief.md` first. Inside a studio run the lines are approved in the
  storyboard review instead of a separate chat approval, and review edits are
  written back to `script.md`.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-05
- Runs the director's intent, insight, and hook steps before writing when it is installed.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-02
- New sub-skill: voiceover script in five parts and three acts, a word budget of
  length × 2.5, a per-act timing table measured from 11 Grumo explainers,
  a line-by-line SUCCESS score, and one line per storyboard scene.
