Rules: pick a light or dark base once and never alternate it. The accent points
at one thing per shot. No gradient base and no neon outer glow; depth comes from
grain or two flat tones. One or two accents may come from real screenshots while
the base stays. On the contact sheet, any shot with a color from "another film" is
a defect.
