# Print, brand kit, and signage sizing

Load this for stationery, minimum logo size, clear space, mockup scale, and
signs. Sources were read 2026-10-07. Brand guides do not share one standard, so
these are defaults to use when the brand has none of its own. A brand's own
guide always wins.

## Minimum size and clear space

| Rule | Default | Range across brand guides |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum print width, wordmark | 25 mm | 30 to 50 mm |
| Minimum print width, symbol | 10 mm | 8 to 10 mm |
| Minimum screen width, wordmark | 120 px | 120 to 180 px |
| Minimum screen width, symbol | 40 px | 40 to 45 px |
| Clear space | 1x the wordmark's cap height on every side | 0.5x to 2x a key letter or element |

Below the minimum, switch from the wordmark to the symbol. Nothing (text, edges,
other logos) enters the clear space.

## Stationery

| Item | Default logo width | Share of the item |
|---|---|---|
| Business card, 85 x 55 mm | 40 mm | 47% of card width |
| Letterhead, A4 | 50 mm (range 40 to 60) | 24% of page width |

## Mockup scale

A mockup logo should match the real product, so its share of the product in
frame equals real logo width divided by real product width. Use the apparel
"Share" column in SKILL.md and the stationery shares above. An oversized logo is
the most common sign of a fake mockup. Use straight-on views for approval shots
and context scenes when scale matters.

## Signage

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Letter height for comfortable reading | 1 inch per 10 feet of viewing distance |
| Example | Read from 150 ft needs 15 in letters. Read across a 30 ft lobby needs 3 in letters |
| Billboards and large outdoor | 4 to 8 in for 100 to 200 ft, 8 to 10 in for 200 to 300 ft, 15 to 20 in for 350 to 500 ft |
| Lobby wall logo or art | 60 to 75% of the wall width, at least 0.75x the reception desk width |

Measure the logo's cap height (or the symbol's height) against the letter
height rule. Sans serif, high contrast, and good light read farther. Thin script
and low contrast need bigger letters.

In a shot prompt, state the sign's size and the viewing distance in words, for
example "a wall logo about 6 feet wide above an 8 foot reception desk, letters
about 10 inches tall".
